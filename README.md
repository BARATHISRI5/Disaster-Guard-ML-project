# 🌍 Disaster Guard — Disaster Prediction & Early Warning System

**Predict Potential Disasters. Alert Nearby Communities. Help Protect Lives.**

Disaster Guard is a machine learning-based project designed to predict potential natural disasters and alert people in nearby areas. The system aims to support early preparedness by identifying possible risks and communicating warnings to people who may be affected.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Predict potential disasters using relevant data.
- Provide early warnings about possible disaster risks.
- Identify nearby people who may be affected.
- Send location-based alert messages to improve preparedness.
- Promote disaster awareness and community safety.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Disaster Prediction:** Analyze available data to identify potential disaster risks.
- **Location-Based Alerts:** Target notifications toward people in potentially affected areas.
- **Alert Messaging:** Send warning messages to help nearby communities prepare.
- **Early Warning Support:** Encourage timely precautions before a potential disaster.
- **Community Awareness:** Improve access to disaster-related information.

1. **Data Collection:** Obtain relevant environmental, weather, or historical data.
